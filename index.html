01 — About

Hard-working, creative and proactive — a teacher who believes every student learns differently, and plans every lesson around that.

02 — Approach

The flipped classroom

Theory moves to home, practice moves to class. Students meet new material before the lesson — so our time together is spent speaking, writing and getting feedback, not listening to a lecture.

iHow a lesson works
01
Before class

Prepare

Short videos, readings and vocabulary sets. Students arrive already knowing the rules and the task format.

02
In class

Practice

Live speaking drills, timed writing tasks, exam-style reading and listening — with instant, specific feedback.

03
After class

Reflect

Corrections, targeted homework and progress tracking, so each lesson builds on the one before.

iiTeaching toolkit

IELTS — all four sections CORE

Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking, taught with my own materials.

Humanistic approach

A supportive atmosphere where students feel safe to speak and make mistakes.

Cambridge curriculum

Structured, well-planned lessons for young learners at an international school.

British Council programme

Specialised training in modern methodology and lesson design.

Differentiated teaching

Different learning styles and needs — one lesson, adapted to each student.

Beginner → Upper-Intermediate

Grammar, vocabulary and productive skills at every level.
